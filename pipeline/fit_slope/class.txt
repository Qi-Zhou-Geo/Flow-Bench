

Volcanic-derived    Volcanic deposits/volcanic terrain constitute a characteristic sediment source

Earthquake-disturbed    A significant earthquake has substantially modified sediment availability through landslides, rockfalls, slope destabilization, etc.

Wildfire-disturbed  Wildfire has substantially modified sediment availability through vegetation/soil disturbance

Hillslope-derived   No major volcanic, seismic, or wildfire disturbance defines the sediment system; sediment is supplied by ongoing hillslope/channel processes
